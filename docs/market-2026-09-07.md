## Review-derived acceptance criteria

Competitor reviews repeatedly expose commercial failure modes that must be treated as requirements, not polish:

1. A choice must create visible, later consequences; avoid "any button gives the same result".
2. No progression-blocking fight-selection, black-screen, roster-loss, or reset bugs.
3. Career records must obey invariants; impossible stats/results destroy trust.
4. Save migration and a recoverable backup path are P0.
5. A paid sports sim needs historical records/statistics and a sense of career ownership.
6. Tactical fights must have readable feedback and spectacle; pure passive watching or repetitive clicking is insufficient.

## Price hypothesis

₩4,400 is a defensible launch hypothesis because multiple current top-five paid Sports products sit at that price. It is not proof that a boxing product will sell at ₩4,400; it is a current willingness-to-pay signal for compact Korean sports simulations.

Do not ship with a title that mechanically copies the current `못하면 또 환생함` naming formula. It would weaken distinctiveness and increase copycat perception. `Twelve Count` is only an internal codename until keyword/store-name validation.
